## Bugs
- **B1 (medium) R-041: a line chip can name the wrong bar.** Ellies City 2 (ACE-Step, heard lines), CHORUS 1, line "Anonymous and free" (sung 48.0–49.32 s). Click it: chip `THIS: BAR 15 · 0:45–0:48`; bar 15 is 44.85–48.05 s, the line is in bar 16 (4 % in bar 15). Cause (*seen in code*, `client/src/chatMark.ts` `markSeconds`): start and end snap to the nearest bar line; when the end snaps to the same line as the start, `to < from` and it marks `barAt(start)`, the bar the line *ends before*. Replaying the snap over the stored reading: 4 of 21 lines have a chip covering under 50 % of the line (also 92.1–94.08 -> bar 29, 38 %; 98.48–100.78 -> bar 31, 33 %; 101.38–103.92 -> bar 32, 42 %). Expected the bar the line mostly sits in. The marked turn would then plan on the wrong bar.
