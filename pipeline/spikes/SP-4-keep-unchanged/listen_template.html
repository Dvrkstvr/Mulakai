SP-4 listen: does an edit keep everything else? ( pairs)

Local files only, not published. Serve this folder (npx http-server . -p 8077, then open http://localhost:8077/index.html): a plain file open or Python's http.server cannot seek in the audio. Pairs 1-20 are the real test; 21-28 are optional extras (healed variants, and two full re-renders for comparison). For each pair, A and B are the song before an edit and after it, in a random order (you are not told which). The after-version was made by keeping the old audio for every bar the edit did not touch and putting the new audio in the changed bars (shaded lilac on the timeline), or, for repeat / cut, by copying or removing bars of the old audio. Press A or B while playing: the switch keeps the same place in the music (where the after-version has an inserted copy, it lands on the bar the copy was taken from). Click or drag the timeline to scrub, focus it and use the arrow keys for one bar, or press a jump button (starts one bar before the spot). Answer, then press reveal to see which one was edited and where the joins are (red marks).
