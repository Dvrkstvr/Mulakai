### (e) edit turns
First-try validity by kind, v3 (2 reps): SET_TEMPO, TRANSPOSE, REPEAT/CUT, EDIT_STYLE **12/12**; REWRITE_LYRICS **1/6** (wrong line count: "block 4 has 8 lines; 4 given", a block/tag mismatch, English words for a German song); REHARMONIZE **2/10** plus the 4 "make it harder / less busy" requests, which are REHARMONIZE too, **0/4**. Experiments on the root-change rule (12 calls each, first-try validity): current prompt 1/12; stronger sentence 3/12; "every second bar" recipe 1/12; a helper field `was` (the old root copied from the bar map before each new root, stripped by code) **6/12** at +40% tokens; in a full run the helper lifted first-try validity to 68.8% but p95 went to 44.9 s, so it is not recommended (`results/extras.json`). Telling the model to write one 16-bar op for a 40-bar section did nothing (3 ops every time). A retry line "keep only the ops the person asked for" changed nothing in 3 seeds.

## Surprises
- The seeds barely matter: both reps chose the same action in 49 of 49 turns; identical retries (same completion tokens three times) show the feedback sometimes changes nothing.
- The model parrots examples (assumptions) and the stored style's numbers (tempo 176 vs the score's 85, 6/8 vs 4/4); the app appends hints to styles that YuE2 does not obey.
- After a TRANSPOSE the model, shown "K:Fm (F minor)", says the key is Gm (6 of 6 times it was checked: v1, v2, v3 x2, v3.1 x2) and only gets it right when code rejects the wrong key; the v1/v2 scores of that turn are wrong in `results.json` because the runner then recorded the stale key.
- Asked "make this part jazzier" with a mark, it adds sax phrases on sung bars; and "give the chorus jazz chords" on a song with three choruses makes all three (24 chords, 47 s) until told to assume the first.
- Enum picks are fine but contradict the prose (`key: A` with "A minor" in the assumptions) — structured fields and free text must not both be shown.
- The root-change rule (D-055) is the single biggest latency cost: 8 of 10 first-try REHARMONIZEs fail it. The first draft of the retry cannot be made cheap; only the helper field helps, at a token cost.
- `lingua` is easy and accurate here (Python); a TypeScript equivalent is an open question, but recipes never needed it.
- Ollama returns identical output for different seeds at temperature 0.3 on a grammar-constrained call more often than I expected (`cap16_test.py`: seeds 21 and 22 gave the same tokens).
