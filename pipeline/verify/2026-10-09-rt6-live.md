## Bugs

1. **Medium: a cover still on its transcription is refused for the word "transcription"** (`ABOUT_READING`, D-263, `retimeReply.ts`): "the transcription read it twice as fast" goes to the reading route and answers `this version has no transcribed reading to re-time`, though the dock's plan works (same turn without the word gives the card). `\btranscri\w*` is the natural word for a cover's source. Reproduce: Kopf hoch getanzt (seed above), send that sentence. Fix direction: only treat "reading / read as" as about the reading, or route "about the reading" to the dock when a dock offer is `offered`.
2. **Medium: the reading route's reply shows a stale view** (`ChatRetimeUndo.tsx` / `chatRetimeTurn.ts`): right after the turn the CHANGED line reads `UNDONE · the reading is back as read` with no UNDO TURN and the READ AS row (65 BPM · 80 bars) and tempo row are not re-timed; the store was re-timed (DB `retime` set). A reload shows the correct `CHANGED · READING · TEMPO, BARS` + UNDO TURN. Cause *inferred*: `retimeTurnLine` returns `done` when the view's `retime` offer exists with `retimed: null` (a view read before the turn), and the re-read in D-264 only fires for `changed`. Seen in 4 of 4 reading re-times.
3. **Low: the planner's sentence and the card disagree by rounding** (`halved to 72.5 BPM` vs card `146.3 → 73`; `within 8 % of the 146 read` next to card `145 → 150`): the sentence uses the song reading's 145, the card the kept bundle's 146.3.
4. **Note, not RT-6**: CP-C1 `--marks` reports 8 of 14 outside on main too (revise carry), so its STOP line no longer measures marks; update the script or its turn list.
