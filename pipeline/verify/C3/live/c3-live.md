## Bugs / observations
- A. After CANCEL on a reading card that was started in a reloaded page, the analyze card stays "READ · STARTING…" (READ disabled) and SEND stays disabled ("SEND waits for the reading and its proposal") until a page reload. Cause: client/src/chatReading.ts:103-104 keeps the previous `starting` phase when the server says `pending` (the server reverts the analyze card to pending on cancel: thread messages analyze pending / reading cancelled).
